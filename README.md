# tapemkt (TapeOut)

> Beginner-friendly crypto trading marketplace by **Panda Capital**. Live at **https://tapemkt.com**

## What is tapemkt

tapemkt (TapeOut) is a trading marketplace built on [tapemkt.com](https://tapemkt.com) and presented by Panda Capital. It is designed to remove the steep learning curve of traditional trading terminals, targeting first-time crypto traders who are intimidated by complex order books and indicator dashboards.

## Highlights

- **3-step onboarding** — sign up, pick an asset, place an order.
- **Clean UI** — surfaces only essential actions, hides advanced noise.
- **One-click order** — first trade in under 5 minutes.
- **Multi-chain assets** supported.
- **Security audit** currently in progress.

## Why it's different

Most trading terminals assume you already know the markets. tapemkt starts from zero: a guided flow that lets non-technical users place their first order without reading a manual.
